DRONE EVENT 🚁

"Innovation Takes Wing"

The Autonomous Drone Challenge is a hands-on technical event emphasizing technical knowledge, practical drone-building skills, and innovation in autonomous navigation. Participants will design, build, and program a drone to navigate autonomously without GPS or external navigation aids, first simulating the dynamics in MATLAB/Simulink and then flying their drone in an arena to scan, detect, and precisely land.

Team Size: 3 Members Registration Fee: 200 INR ← Back to Events

Event Structure

Round 1: Simulation Modelling

1 hour

Simulink simulation challenge for all 100 teams. Points: 10.

Round 2: Scan Zone Arena

1.5 hours

Autonomous Drone Arena Challenge - Detection & Navigation. Points: 15.

Round 3: Pathfinder Navigation

1.5-2 hours

Autonomous Navigation & Precision for top 50% teams. Points: 25.

Final Round: Grand Challenge

1-1.5 hours

Combines Scan Zone and Pathfinder elements for top 5-10 teams. Winners decided.

Rules & Regulations

Team Composition: Teams must consist of exactly 3 participants.
Each team is responsible for designing, building, and programming their own drone.
Teams must bring their own drone for all physical flight rounds.
Drone Requirements: No GPS or external navigation aids are allowed; drones must operate fully autonomously.
Drones must be safe to operate indoors or in the designated arena.
Teams must ensure drones are in flight-ready condition at the start of each round.
Scoring: Round 1 (10 points), Round 2 (15 points), Round 3 (25 points). Total: 50 points.
Filtering: Top 50% of teams from Rounds 1 & 2 advance to Round 3.
Final Round: Top 5-10 teams compete for prizes based on cumulative points.
Use of external help or pre-programmed solutions outside the team is prohibited.
Teams must stay within the designated arena during drone flights.
Judges' decisions are final and binding.
Registration fee is 200 INR per team.
