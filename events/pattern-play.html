PATTERN PLAY 🖼️🌀

Unleash your imagination through chance and repetition

Pattern Play is an interactive art event that invites participants to discover creativity through spontaneity and repetition. Using stencils, stamps, or everyday objects, participants generate abstract compositions that transform random impressions into meaningful visual patterns. The event celebrates the playful side of design, encouraging participants to experiment with textures and embrace unpredictability.

Team Size: 2 Members Registration Fee: 500 INR ← Back to Events

Event Structure

Spontaneous Creation – Prelims

Round 1

Objective: Translate randomness into an abstract pattern. Choose from given themes: Chaos to Order or Rhythm of Lines. Time limit: 1.5 hours.

Thematic Refinement – Finals

Round 2

A surprise theme will be given on the spot (e.g., Nature Prints, Urban Impressions, Symmetry in Motion). Time limit: 2 hours. Participants must refine their artwork into a cohesive thematic piece.

Rules & Regulations

Eligibility: Open to all students; individual or team of 2 members permitted.
Materials: Basic tools (stencils, stamps, sponges, and sheets) will be provided. Participants may bring additional simple objects for stamping or texturing (e.g., leaves, coins, bottle caps).
Originality: Plagiarism or use of pre-made molds/templates is prohibited.
Judging Criteria: Creativity, interpretation of theme, composition, and innovation in material use.
Jury Decision: The decision of the jury panel will be final and binding.
