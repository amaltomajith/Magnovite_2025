REVERSE CODING 🎨💻

"Programming beyond limits, creating beyond rules"

Reverse Coding challenges participants to transform abstract ideas, emotions, or artistic concepts into code, resulting in a visual or auditory output that captures the essence of a given "vibe" or theme. It celebrates the fusion of art and technology.

Event Structure

Preliminaries

60 minutes

A 60-minute round where teams create a small-scale output interpreting a theme/vibe.

Finals

90–120 minutes

A 90–120 minute round for developing a full-fledged, interactive piece of art, an animated sequence, or a generative music track.

Rules & Regulations

Allowed languages and frameworks are flexible. Internet usage is allowed for referencing libraries and documentation.
Submissions must be runnable and self-contained.
All work must be original and created during the event. Plagiarism or unauthorized external assistance is strictly forbidden.
Team size must be between 1-3 members only.
Each team must pay a registration fee of 300 INR.
All participants must bring their own laptops and necessary software.
Participants must ensure their code runs smoothly and produces the intended output.
Judges decision will be final and binding.
Any misconduct or rule violation will lead to immediate disqualification.
