CASECRAFT : THE INDUSTRY EDITION 💼⚙️

"Solving real-world challenges with technical insight"

CaseCraft is a dynamic case competition built around two critical domains: technical problem-solving and business strategy. Participants will tackle real-world challenges that test their analytical skills while driving practically-oriented strategic decision-making. By bringing these domains together, CaseCraft fosters innovation, holistic thinking, and solutions that are both technically robust and strategically impactful.

Team Size: 2–4 Members Registration Fee: 400 INR/Team

Event Structure (2 Rounds)

Pitch Deck Submission: Each team submits their final pitch deck strictly limited to 7 slides (exclusive of title slide and annexures); any excess content will not be considered.
Final Presentation: The top 10 teams shortlisted from each domain (technical and business/strategy) in the submission rounds will present their solutions in person to a panel of judges, inclusive of industry experts and organisation representatives, and academic judges.

Rules & Regulations

Each team must consist of 2 to 4 currently enrolled students from any recognized postgraduate or undergraduate program.
Mixed-discipline teams (across technical and business domains) are encouraged.
Each participant may only be part of one team.
Submissions must be original work created during the competition period and must not feature any identifying university names, logos, or personal details.
Respectful, professional behavior is expected throughout the event toward all participants, coordinators, and judges.
Judges’ decisions are final and binding in all matters related to rankings and awards.
The organizing committee reserves the right to adjust the event structure, schedule, or rules as necessary.
