SEVERANCE CUP 🏆🎮

"Compete, conquer, and claim the crown!"

The Severance Cup is an inter-collegiate esports tournament featuring competitions in two games: Valorant and EA FC (EFOOTBALL). A points system is used to calculate the performance of each college across both games, and the institution with the highest cumulative score is declared the overall winner of the tournament.

Event Structure

Valorant Tournament

5+1 Players per team

Single elimination best of 1 match till finals. Final is best of 3. BYOC policy applies.

EFOOTBALL Tournament

1-2 Players per college

1v1 single elimination knockout bracket on EA Sports FIFA with 6-minute halves.

Rules & Regulations

College Contingent Size: Must field participants in both events - one Valorant team (5+1) and 1-2 EFOOTBALL players.
All members must be full-time students from the same institution.
All participants must present valid, current college-issued photo ID card for verification.
Both tournaments are single-elimination knockout format running simultaneously.
Championship Scoring: 1st (100 pts), 2nd (60 pts), 3rd-4th (30 pts each), 5th-8th (10 pts each).
Valorant: BYOC policy - players must bring complete setup including laptop/PC, monitor, peripherals.
Valorant: Teams must be ready 15 minutes before scheduled match with 10-minute grace period.
EFOOTBALL: Tournament played on EA Sports FIFA on organizers' consoles with standard controllers.
EFOOTBALL: Team selection limited to current Clubs or International teams. Classic XI and All-Star teams banned.
Code of Conduct: Aggression, harassment, toxicity, or cheating leads to immediate disqualification.
Schedule/Bracket Draw: Random draw conducted 3 days prior to event start date.
Tie-Breaker: College with higher individual placement (1st place finish) wins.
Registration fee is 600 INR per college contingent.
Decisions of tournament administrators are final and binding.
