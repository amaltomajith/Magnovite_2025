Event Structure

Round 1: Foundational Knowledge

MCQ Assessment

Customized MCQs covering power system fundamentals and crisis management principles.

Round 2: Visual Analysis

Fault Identification

Visual analysis and logical reasoning tasks using randomized fault identification scenarios on power distribution networks.

Round 3: Collaborative Analysis

Decision Making

Collaborative scenario analysis focusing on decision-making for typical blackout causes with low-pressure team dynamics.

Round 4: Crisis Simulation

High-Intensity

High-intensity, time-bound multi-layered crisis simulation with resource constraints, information gaps, and dynamic surprise elements.

Round 5: Championship

Final Presentation

Comprehensive restoration plan development and live presentation with expert panel Q&A session.

Rules & Regulations

Team Composition: Exactly 4 members per team with active participation mandatory from all members.
Device Policy: Electronic devices (smartphones, tablets, calculators) permitted only when explicitly authorized by event facilitators for specific rounds.
Time Management: Strict adherence to round-wise time limits is required.
Content Integrity: Each team receives unique, randomized question sets, scenarios, and challenges to ensure originality and prevent collaboration between teams.
Professional Conduct: Respectful behavior toward organizers, judges, and fellow participants is required; violations may lead to disqualification.
Judging Process: Transparent evaluation based on predefined criteria including accuracy, creativity, teamwork, presentation skills, and crisis management capability.
Decision Authority: Organizing committee and judges hold final authority on all competition-related decisions.
Team size must be between 4-5 members.
Registration fee is 500 INR per team.
Unauthorized device usage results in penalties.
