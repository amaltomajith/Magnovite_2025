FRAMES UNBOXED 🎬💡

"Where Insights Meet Imagination"

Frames Unboxed is a short film-making competition where participants express their creativity through videography and filmmaking. Teams design, film, and submit a short film based on a theme revealed during the event.

Team Size: 6–8 Fee: 500 INR/Team ← Back to Events

Event Structure (Two-Day Event)

Day 1: Briefing, theme reveal, and start of filming (on campus until 09:00 PM).
Day 2: Submission of films online in the morning.

Rules & Regulations

Duration of the short film: 7–8 minutes.
Number of participants per team: 6–8 participants.
Participants must bring their own technical equipment.
All filming must take place within the campus premises.
All content must be original. Plagiarism will lead to immediate disqualification.
The film must include the given soundtrack as well as any others used.
Soundtracks must be free of vulgar or derogatory language.
No obscene, vulgar, or inappropriate scenes are permitted.
No submissions will be accepted after the deadline.
The specific themes will be revealed on the day of the event.
