THE NEXUS ⚔️🤝

"Every Alliance is a Gamble. Every Battle is a Weapon."

The Nexus is a negotiation-based strategy event where teams representing rival factions, with limited resources and a secret mission, must survive by trading, forming alliances, and adapting to unexpected challenges. The aim is to survive by revealing, persuading, and strategically decision-making under pressure.

Team Size: 4 Fee: 400 INR ← Back to Events

Event Structure (3 Rounds spanning 2 days)

Round 1: Prelims - Survival Strategy Quiz (20 questions per participant). Top 24 qualify.
Round 2: The Negotiation Arena (5 Sub-Rounds: team formation, three negotiation rounds with curveballs, two presentation rounds).
Round 3: Results & Awards - Scoring based on mission completion, resources, alliance stability, and strategy.

Rules & Regulations

Event phases: Preliminary Round, Negotiation Arena, Final Round.
Teams of 4 with fixed roles: Leader, Trader, Spy, Strategist.
All trades/negotiations go through the Game Master; secret deals via written notes.
Curveballs announced are mandatory and final; betrayals allowed but must be revealed in presentations.
No obscene, vulgar, or inappropriate language; violations lead to disqualification.
