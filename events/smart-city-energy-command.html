SMART CITY ENERGY COMMAND 💡🏙️

"Where Strategy Meets Sustainability"

Smart City Energy Command is an innovative interdisciplinary simulation competition where teams of four from diverse academic backgrounds collaborate as city energy management consultants. The event combines strategic decision-making, policy analysis, economic optimization, and stakeholder management while balancing technical feasibility, social impact, and environmental sustainability.

Event Structure

Round 1: Urban Energy Literacy

1 hour

Multi-choice Quiz covering energy basics, sustainability, economics, and policy. Top 60 teams advance.

Round 2: Stakeholder Analysis

1.5 hours

Case Study Analysis - analyze needs and propose solutions. Top 30 teams advance.

Round 3: Resource Allocation

1 hour

Strategic Planning Challenge - distribute limited resources across city sectors. Top 15 teams advance.

Round 4: Multi-Crisis Management

1.5 hours

Simulation handling simultaneous crises: budget cuts, public opposition, technical failures. Top 8 teams advance.

Round 5: City Transformation Pitch

1.5 hours

Present comprehensive 10-year energy transition plan with Q&A. Winners decided.

Rules & Regulations

Final Round Evaluation: Based on Technical soundness (25%), Economic viability (25%), Social impact (25%), and Environmental sustainability (25%).
Use of external help is prohibited.
Electronic devices allowed only during designated research phases.
Judge's decision is final.
Teams must collaborate effectively and present unified solutions.
Professional conduct mandatory throughout the event.
Team size must be 2 to 4 members with interdisciplinary composition strongly encouraged.
Registration fee ranges from 200-300 INR per team.
Teams must navigate energy planning scenarios, crisis management, and propose comprehensive long-term plans.
Any misconduct or rule violation will lead to immediate disqualification.
Each institution can have a maximum of two registrations as contingents for every event
