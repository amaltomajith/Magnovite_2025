Mechanical Engineering RC Robot Chase

MAZE RUNNER 🏃‍♂️🤖

"Catch the target, master the maze!"

The Maze Runner is an adrenaline-filled RC robot chase competition. The arena is designed as a maze where two teams face off simultaneously: one robot plays the Target (whose goal is to evade capture), while the other is the Hunter (whose objective is to catch the Target within a set time limit of 3 minutes). Roles are decided by a coin toss before each match. The event emphasizes technical design, control precision, speed, and strategy.

Event Structure

Bot Registration & Inspection

Technical Check

Teams register their robots and undergo technical inspection to ensure compliance with specifications.

Maze Chase Matches

3 minutes per match

Two teams face off simultaneously - one robot as Target (evade capture) and other as Hunter (catch Target). Roles decided by coin toss.

Rules & Regulations

Each team must consist of a minimum of 2 and a maximum of 5 members.
Members must carry a valid student ID card.
Team members need not belong to the same college/university.
The event will be canceled if fewer than three teams register.
Only one bot per team is allowed.
The bot should not have sharp elements or cause damage to the arena.
The bot must fit within a cube of 25 cm × 25 cm × 25 cm (including wheels).
Bots must be wirelessly controlled.
Only voltage up to 12V can be used at any point in the bot.
Wheels or mechanisms that may damage the arena are not permitted.
No external assistance (like wires, external guiding mechanisms, or drones) is allowed.
Registration fee is 300 INR per team.
Each match has a time limit of 3 minutes.
Roles (Target/Hunter) are decided by a coin toss before each match.
The event emphasizes technical design, control precision, speed, and strategy.
